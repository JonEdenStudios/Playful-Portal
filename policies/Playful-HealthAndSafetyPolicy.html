01

Policy statement

Playful Ltd is committed to providing a safe and healthy working environment for all employees, contractors and anyone else who may be affected by our work. We take our legal responsibilities seriously and will do everything reasonably practicable to prevent accidents, injury and ill health.

This policy applies to all Playful employees and to contractors and Playmakers working on Playful projects. It covers all settings in which Playful operates — including home working, hired meeting spaces, client premises, venues and installation sites.

02

Scope

This policy covers:

  • All three Playful employees, working primarily from home
  • All contractors and Playmakers engaged by Playful
  • Visitors and members of the public in spaces where Playful is operating

It applies across all working contexts: home offices, shared or hired meeting spaces, site visits to venues and cultural spaces, installation and activation work, and client meetings on or off site.

03

Responsibilities

  • Beth CooperNamed Health & Safety lead — policy owner, risk assessment oversight, incident reporting

All employees, contractors and Playmakers are responsible for:

  • Taking reasonable care of their own health and safety and that of others
  • Following any site-specific safety rules or instructions
  • Reporting hazards, accidents or near misses to Beth Cooper promptly
  • Not misusing or interfering with anything provided for health and safety purposes
04

Home working

Playful’s founders and many of its contractors work from home. While Playful cannot inspect home working environments directly, all employees are expected to:

  • Set up a safe, comfortable working environment with appropriate seating, lighting and screen positioning
  • Take regular breaks from screen-based work
  • Ensure their home working space is free from trip hazards and other foreseeable risks
  • Report any work-related health concerns — including repetitive strain, eye strain or stress — to Beth Cooper

Playful will provide guidance on home workstation setup on request.

05

Site visits and installation work

Site visits to venues, cultural spaces, heritage sites and public locations are a regular part of Playful’s work. Before and during site visits, employees, contractors and Playmakers should:

  • Familiarise themselves with the venue’s own health and safety procedures, including emergency exits and assembly points
  • Follow all instructions from venue staff and site managers
  • Wear appropriate footwear and clothing for the environment
  • Not attempt to move, lift or install equipment beyond their physical capability — ask for assistance
  • Report any unsafe conditions to the venue contact and to Beth Cooper

For installation and activation work involving physical setup of products (Playshapes, Playwall, Playbox etc.), specific handling guidance is available from Beth Cooper before each project.

06

Manual handling

Some Playful products — particularly Playshapes foam cubes and Playwall panels — require physical handling during setup and breakdown. Anyone involved in this work should:

  • Plan lifts before attempting them — assess the weight, route and destination
  • Use the Wheelboard trolley system where available rather than carrying by hand
  • Ask for help with anything awkward or heavy rather than attempting it alone
  • Never rush setup or breakdown in a way that increases risk of injury
07

Hired and shared meeting spaces

When using hired or shared meeting rooms, employees should satisfy themselves that the space is safe and suitable before use. Any concerns about the condition of a hired space should be raised with the venue and noted to Beth Cooper.

08

Lone working

Playful employees and contractors sometimes work alone — on site visits, during setup, or at events. Lone workers should:

  • Inform a colleague or the venue contact of their whereabouts and expected return time
  • Keep a charged mobile phone with them at all times
  • Not carry out high-risk activities alone where possible

A separate Lone Working Policy supplements this section with more detailed guidance.

09

Accidents and incidents

If an accident requires emergency services, call 999 first.

All accidents, injuries and near misses — however minor — must be reported to Beth Cooper as soon as practicable. Beth will:

  • Record the incident in Playful’s accident log
  • Determine whether RIDDOR reporting is required
  • Review whether any change to practice or procedure is needed
10

Risk assessments

Playful will carry out risk assessments for activities that present a foreseeable risk of harm — particularly installation and activation work in public spaces, and any work involving children or members of the public.

Risk assessments will be carried out before work begins on relevant projects, reviewed following any incident or significant change to the work, and kept on file and available to relevant team members.

11

Stress and mental health

Playful recognises that work-related stress and poor mental health are legitimate health and safety concerns. Employees and contractors are encouraged to raise any concerns about workload, working conditions or wellbeing with Beth Cooper in confidence. Playful will take such concerns seriously and respond appropriately.

12

Policy review

This policy will be reviewed annually, or sooner following any significant incident or change in working practice.
