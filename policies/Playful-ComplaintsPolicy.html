01

Policy statement

Playful is committed to delivering high-quality play experiences and working professionally with everyone we engage with. We take complaints seriously and aim to resolve them fairly, promptly and transparently. This policy sets out how to raise a complaint and how we will handle it.

02

Scope

This policy applies to anyone who has cause to raise a concern about Playful’s work, conduct or services — including clients, venue partners, collaborators, members of the public and anyone who has experienced one of our installations or events.

03

How to raise a complaint

Complaints should be submitted in writing to Dirk Mischendahl at dirk.mischendahl@makeitplayful.com.

Please include:

  • Your name and contact details
  • A clear description of the complaint
  • When the issue occurred
  • Any relevant supporting information

We will acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days of receipt.

04

How we handle complaints

On receipt, Dirk will review the complaint and, where necessary, speak with other members of the team to gather relevant information. We aim to provide a full written response within 20 working days. If we need longer, we will contact you to explain why and give a revised timeframe.

We will treat all complaints confidentially and with respect.

05

Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our response, or if your complaint relates directly to Dirk Mischendahl, you may request that it be reviewed by one of the other Playful founders. If the complaint cannot be resolved internally, we may recommend an independent third party to assist.

06

Review

This policy is owned by Dirk Mischendahl and will be reviewed annually.
