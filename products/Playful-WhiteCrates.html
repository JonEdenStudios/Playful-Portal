Who they are

White Crates is a collaborator offering bespoke container architecture and modular building design and build. They work at the intersection of temporary and permanent architecture, using shipping containers as a primary building material.

White Crates is not a Playful product — it is a specialist design and build partner that Playful can bring in where a brief calls for a container-based structure.

02

What they do

White Crates operates as a creative studio and architectural practice. Every project is bespoke. They tailor their response to the brief, budget, site, timescale and stakeholders, working with developers, main contractors, architects, local authorities and transport specialists to find the most efficient delivery path.

Their process covers design through to production. Units are fabricated at their own dedicated facility and delivered ready for site — in most cases plug and play.

Relationship to Playful’s offer

White Crates is most relevant when a client brief calls for a container-based built structure — permanent, semi-permanent or temporary. This could include a dedicated play building or pavilion, a café or retail unit integrated with a play installation, a container-based activation structure for a cultural venue, or any brief where Playbox is too small in scope.

Their track record with major venues and local authorities strengthens Playful’s position in larger, more complex commissions.

05

Key notes for proposals

  • White Crates is a collaborator, not a Playful product — position them as a specialist partner brought in for the right brief.
  • Every project is bespoke — there are no standard prices. Engage White Crates early for any brief that includes a container building element.
  • Their track record includes high-profile venues (National Railway Museum, Liverpool FC) — useful to reference with institutional clients.
  • The plug-and-play delivery model is worth highlighting for clients with tight timelines or complex sites.
