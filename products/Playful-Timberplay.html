01

Who they are

Timberplay is one of the UK’s most respected playground equipment suppliers and designers, operating across the UK, Middle East and India. Founded in 2001, they began with one of the world’s most prestigious play commissions — the Princess Diana Memorial Playground in Kensington Gardens — and have built a significant international track record since.

They are the exclusive UK, Middle East and India supplier of Richter Spielgeräte, a German manufacturer with over 750 play products. They also hold exclusive partnerships with other leading public realm manufacturers. Alongside product supply, they have their own in-house team of Landscape Architects.

Website: timberplay.com  ·  Markets: UK · UAE · Saudi Arabia · Qatar · India

02

The agreement with Playful

Playful and Timberplay have a two-way commercial agreement.

1. Timberplay as Playshapes distributor (non-exclusive) — Timberplay can sell and supply Playshapes to their clients through their worldwide network. This gives Playshapes reach into markets — particularly the Middle East and India — that Playful would not access directly. The arrangement is non-exclusive on both sides.

2. Playful recommending and co-working with Timberplay — Where a client brief calls for outdoor playground equipment, permanent play structures or landscape-led play design, Playful can recommend Timberplay and work alongside them to deliver the full project. Timberplay brings the outdoor, structural, and landscape expertise; Playful brings the indoor and activation layer.

03

What Timberplay does

Products — Over 750 products from Richter Spielgeräte alone, plus ranges from other exclusive manufacturer partners. Covering:

  • Adventure play — climbing structures, towers, rope forests, suspension bridges
  • Sand and water play
  • Swings and movement equipment
  • Seesaws and rocking equipment
  • Themed and bespoke play structures
  • Inclusive play equipment
  • Natural play — robinia and larch timber structures

All equipment manufactured to EN1176 and EN1177 European safety standards. Materials are sustainably sourced — naturally durable timbers including Robinia and Mountain Larch.

Services

  • Play space design led by in-house Landscape Architects
  • Bespoke product design with specialist focus on inclusive design
  • Project management and installation (certified teams)
  • Maintenance contracts and aftercare
  • CPD sessions for specifiers, architects and councils

Design philosophy — Timberplay are guided by Play Value — the amount of fun and developmental benefit a child can get from a piece of equipment or a combination of elements. They take a landscape-led approach, integrating play into the natural environment rather than placing equipment on top of it.

05

Relationship to Playful’s offer

Timberplay and Playful operate in complementary spaces. Timberplay’s core strength is outdoor, permanent, structural play — fixed equipment, landscape design, large-scale public park and school playground projects. Playful’s strength is indoor and activation play — Playshapes, Playwall, artist-led products, cultural venue programming.

The relationship creates mutual value:

  • Timberplay clients planning indoor or soft play elements can be introduced to Playshapes
  • Playful clients wanting outdoor permanent play structures can be referred to Timberplay
  • Joint proposals are possible on larger mixed-brief projects covering both indoor and outdoor elements
  • The international distribution network gives Playshapes reach in markets Playful doesn’t currently serve directly
06

Key notes for proposals

  • When a brief has an outdoor permanent play element, Timberplay is the natural referral. Bring them in early on landscape-led or park projects.
  • Their Middle East track record (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar) is significant — if Playful ever has enquiries from those regions, Timberplay is the right delivery partner.
  • The Richter Spielgeräte partnership is worth knowing about — Richter is very well regarded among landscape architects and public realm specifiers. Being associated with Timberplay carries credibility in that world.
  • For joint proposals, agree clearly upfront which organisation leads the client relationship and how fees/margins are structured.
