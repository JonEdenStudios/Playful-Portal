RefoPLAY — what it is

RefoPLAY is a playground safety shock pad system made from recycled agglomerated polyethylene (PE) foam. Manufactured by APETEK SRL (Italy) and supplied in the UK through Kewell Converters — the same supplier as the Plastazote foam used in Playshapes.

Interlocking foam tiles that sit beneath a surface layer — synthetic grass, rubber matting or similar — to provide impact attenuation and fall protection. Vertical drainage structure allows water to pass through (>3,000mm/h), preventing pooling.

Tested to EN 1177:2018 (European standard for impact-attenuating playground surfacing). APETEK is an ESTC full member.

02

RefoPLAY — what it’s suited to

  • Outdoor playgrounds, parks and public play spaces requiring certified fall protection
  • Any installation where the critical fall height of the play equipment determines the required shock pad thickness
  • Projects where drainage performance matters — the vertical drainage system handles >3,000mm/h

This is a sub-surface safety layer, not a stand-alone play surface. Always used in combination with a top surface — typically synthetic grass, rubber matting or similar.

SBR40 Rubber Tile — what it is

The SBR40 is a heavy-duty recycled rubber tile used by Playful as a temporary, deployable safety surface for play activations. Made from recycled SBR rubber granules (Styrene-Butadiene Rubber, typically from end-of-life tyres) bonded with polyurethane glue. Robust, portable and available in a choice of colours.

Unlike RefoPLAY (which sits beneath another surface layer), the SBR40 is the finished play surface itself — laid directly onto concrete or a reinforced sub-base and removed after the activation. Its weight and durability make it well suited to repeated deployment.

Manufactured by MFL Group s.r.o. (Czech Republic), trading under the More for Less brand.
