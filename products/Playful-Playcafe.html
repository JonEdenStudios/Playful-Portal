01

What it is

Playcafé is a converted shipping container — self-contained, road-deliverable, and ready to trade. One unit. Two distinct halves. Everything it needs built in.

One side is a fully equipped café outlet: coffee machine, drinks fridge, ice-cream freezer and till. Signage, lighting and a fold-out awning for outdoor cover are included. The unit runs on solar-charged batteries — with a swappable battery system designed for sites without a mains connection. Temporary water supply and handwashing waste are built in.

The other half is configurable. Supervised play space with overnight equipment storage. Additional café seating. Or specified differently to suit the client’s programme. The second half does whatever the brief needs it to do.

Playcafé is being developed in partnership with White Crate, Playful’s container architecture collaborators, whose track record includes the National Railway Museum café and Liverpool FC street food units.

02

The two halves

The container divides cleanly into two equal sections. Each is designed for its own purpose — but the two work as a single unit on site.

Fixed

Café outlet

  • Espresso coffee machine
  • Drinks fridge
  • Ice-cream freezer
  • Till and payment terminal
  • Integrated signage and lighting
  • Fold-out awning for outdoor cover
Configurable

Second half

  • Supervised play space with overnight equipment storage
  • Additional café tables and seating
  • Any other client-specific use — specify at brief stage

When configured as a play space, the second half stores play equipment inside the closed unit overnight — solving a common operational problem for venues running supervised play programmes without a permanent building for kit.

04

What it’s suited to

Playcafé works wherever a capable, commercial unit needs to be dropped in without relying on permanent infrastructure.

Good fit

  • Public parks and green spaces with no utility connection
  • Cultural venue grounds and outdoor sites
  • Festival and event environments — short or extended stays
  • School holiday and seasonal pop-up activations
  • Any site where a fixed café build is not viable but the need for family catering is real
  • Venues running supervised play programmes needing overnight kit storage

Less suited

  • Permanent indoor installations — this is an outdoor, temporary or semi-permanent product
  • Sites with no vehicle access for delivery
  • Very small footprints — the unit plus awning needs adequate clearance

The play-space configuration makes Playcafé well matched to family-facing venues. Parents can be served on one side while children are engaged on the other. Dwell time and spend naturally follow.
