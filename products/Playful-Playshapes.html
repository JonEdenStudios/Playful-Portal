01

What it is

Playshapes is a large-scale free play system designed by artist Pippa Hale and manufactured and supplied by Playful Ltd. Product website: playshapes.co.uk.

Made from Plastazote LD45 (Zotefoam) — a closed cell, cross-linked low density polyethylene foam. Fire retardant (passes FMVSS.302), toxic safe, and water resistant (<1% absorption). Precision-cut and processed by Kewell Convertors.

Supplied as a set of 6 cubes. Each cube packs down to 900 × 900 × 900mm. Pieces can be taken apart and used in any combination — stacked, arranged, climbed on, built with — then packed away again. No instruction, no fixed outcome. Designed for open-ended free play across all ages.

04

What it’s suited to

Good fit

  • Museums, galleries and heritage sites with family or mixed-age audiences
  • Cultural venues running temporary or seasonal play activations
  • Public atria, foyers and entrance halls with sufficient floor area
  • Libraries, children’s centres and community venues
  • Clients wanting a premium, artist-designed play product

Less suited

  • Outdoor use — foam is weather-sensitive
  • Very small spaces — a full set needs meaningful floor area
  • Clients with very tight budgets
05

Typical scale and footprint

A standard set packs into 6 units of 900 × 900 × 900mm. In active play, pieces spread across a significantly larger area — a typical activation works well in 30–60m² of floor space. The system scales up by adding sets. Individual cubes can also be deployed independently.

Bespoke & custom

Bespoke designs are available in collaboration with Pippa Hale — covering custom shapes, modified forms and colourways outside the standard palette. Bespoke work is quoted individually and agreed directly with the artist. Lead times will be longer than standard.

Position the bespoke route for clients with a genuine brief for something unique, or where the standard set doesn’t fit the space or brand.

08

Key notes for proposals

  • Playshapes photographs well — strong win for client communications and social media.
  • The “no installation” story is valuable for venues with complex access or listed buildings.
  • The hire option opens the conversation for clients who can’t commit to a purchase.
  • Artist-designed provenance is a differentiator in cultural venue contexts.
  • Multiple sets can be combined for larger spaces or higher footfall environments.
  • Always include the Wheelboard Set — it solves a real operational problem and is low cost relative to the product.
