02

What we collect and why

Enquiries and business contacts

When you contact us — by email, through our website or in person — we collect your name, email address, organisation and any other details you choose to share. We use this to respond to you and to manage our client relationships. Our legal basis is legitimate interests.

Project and client work

If you commission work from us, we hold the personal data necessary to deliver and manage that project — contact details, correspondence and relevant project information. Our legal basis is contract performance.

Photography and video

Playful's work is documented through photography and video for use in our portfolio, marketing materials, social media and press. This documentation routinely includes images of people — including children — in our installations.

Where images are taken in publicly accessible venues or installations, we rely on legitimate interests as our legal basis. Where we have greater control over the context — a private event, a school, or a venue where we have direct access to attendees — we seek appropriate consent, including parental or guardian consent for images of children under 18.

If you do not want yourself or your child to appear in our photography, please speak to the venue or a member of the Playful team on the day. We will always respect that request.

Website visitors

Our website collects standard technical data through cookies and analytics — including IP addresses, browser type, pages visited and time on site. We use this to understand how the site performs and to improve it. See the Cookies section for more detail.

Email marketing

If you have opted in to receive updates from us, we hold your name and email address for this purpose. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send, or by contacting us at admin@makeitplayful.com.

How we share your data

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to run our business. Each acts as a data processor on our behalf and is bound by appropriate data processing agreements:

  • Monday.comClient relationship and project management
  • Email marketing platformCommunications to opted-in contacts
  • Google WorkspaceEmail, documents and internal collaboration
  • XeroFinancial records and accounts
  • Website hosting providersHosting and infrastructure

We choose providers that meet UK GDPR standards. Where data is transferred outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

We may also share data where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of Playful, our clients or the public.

05

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Restrict how we process your data, under certain conditions
  • Object to how we process your data, under certain conditions
  • Request that we transfer your data to another organisation, or directly to you, under certain conditions
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are not satisfied with how we have handled your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@makeitplayful.com. We will respond within one calendar month.

06

Children's data

Playful works in spaces visited by children, and our installations are frequently photographed with children present. We handle images of children with particular care.

We do not collect personal data directly from children. Where we have control over the context in which photography is taken, we seek parental or guardian consent before capturing or using images of children under 18.

If you believe an image of your child has been used without appropriate consent, contact us at admin@makeitplayful.com and we will review it promptly.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small files stored on your device — to help the site work and to understand how visitors use it.

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the site to function correctly
  • Analytics cookies — to understand traffic and usage patterns

When you first visit the site, you will be asked to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can update your preferences at any time through your browser settings.
