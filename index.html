02 / About
About Playful.
Who we are
We design and build play for public spaces.

Playful is a Leeds-based creative company specialising in interactive play installations and visitor experiences. We work with museums, galleries, heritage sites, cultural venues and public spaces — anywhere that wants to give people a real reason to engage.

We're not an events company. We're not a soft play supplier. We design and manufacture physical interactive experiences that are built to last, built to perform and built to change how people feel about a space.

Play isn't decorative. It drives dwell time, deepens engagement and brings people back. That's the business case — and it's one we've made work for clients across the UK.

What we make
Products built for real spaces and real people.

Our product range runs from compact modular units through to large-scale bespoke commissions. Everything is designed and made in-house at our Leeds workshop, which means we control quality at every stage.
